# FAQ
Skills are instructions; runtimes execute. Complex rendered animation internals require editing HTML/React and re-rendering. The visible browser timeline must match the delivered export. No accounts or private example media are included.

## 换电脑为什么剪辑方式不同，怎样保持一致？

GitHub保存规则与源码；插件连接、登录、素材和工程状态须在本机另查。默认ChatCut主时间轴，缺连接不能自动整片改成本机。改单个音效需独立事件片段，整条混音不能逐个选择。详见[26_GitHub_Skill_Integration.md](26_GitHub_Skill_Integration.md)。
